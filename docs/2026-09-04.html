Deglobalization & Supply Chains
Trade blocs are replacing free trade. Nearshoring, friendshoring, CHIPS Act subsidies, and export controls are forcing companies to rebuild supply chains for resilience over efficiency. This structural shift is persistently inflationary (+0.5–1% CPI), distorts FX rates, and creates long-duration capex cycles in manufacturing, semiconductors, and energy infrastructure.
