Private Credit Boom
Private credit has grown into a $2T+ market, replacing banks as the primary lenders to mid-market companies. PE firms (Apollo, Ares, Blackstone) now control lending that banks once dominated. This shifts credit risk off regulated balance sheets and into less transparent structures.
