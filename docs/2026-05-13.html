Canadian Economy & Bank of Canada
Canada's economy is uniquely exposed to US trade policy, commodity cycles, and a heavily indebted consumer. The Bank of Canada often diverges from the Fed — when it does, CAD/USD moves and cross-border capital flows become the trade.
